## 🧠 Business Problem  

Employee attrition is not just an HR metric—it directly impacts **cost, productivity, and team stability**.  

The organization faced:  
- Unpredictable attrition across departments  
- No clarity on **who is at risk**  
- No visibility into **why employees are leaving**  

👉 Result: **Reactive decisions instead of proactive strategy**  

---

## 🎯 Objective  

Build an interactive dashboard to:  
- 🔍 Identify high-risk employees  
- 📊 Uncover key attrition drivers  
- 💡 Enable data-driven HR decisions  

---

## ⚙️ Approach  

### 1️⃣ Data Preparation  
- Cleaned missing & inconsistent data  
- Created derived features:  
  - Salary slabs  
  - Tenure groups  
  - Attrition flags  

### 2️⃣ Analysis  
- Segmented attrition by demographics  
- Evaluated impact of:  
  - 💰 Salary  
  - ⏱️ Overtime  
  - 🧑‍💼 Job role  
  - 📅 Tenure  

### 3️⃣ Visualization  
- Designed Power BI dashboard for **non-technical stakeholders**  
- Focused on **decision-making, not just charts**  

---

## 📊 Key Insights  

- 📉 **Low salary = High attrition risk**  
- ⏱️ **Overtime strongly increases attrition**  
- 📅 **0–2 years tenure = highest churn zone**  
- 🏢 **Sales & HR departments most affected**  

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations  

- 💰 Adjust compensation for high-risk segments  
- ⏱️ Reduce overtime or introduce incentives  
- 🎯 Focus retention programs on early-tenure employees  
- 🏢 Investigate high-attrition departments  

---

## 📈 Impact  

This dashboard helps:  
- Identify attrition risk early  
- Reduce hiring & training costs  
- Improve workforce planning  
- Shift HR from **reactive → proactive**
